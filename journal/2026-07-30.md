0.67x ATR meant SPY needed 5.77 points against an 8.55-point average daily
range — a normal session's move. The contract only had to travel ~3.4 points to
double, which is what happened.

### Entry timing — the five minutes that made the trade

At 08:35 the setup looked ready and I nearly bought. The tape said no: SPY was
oscillating *on* VWAP (735.50) with volume declining bar over bar
(277k → 130k → 137k → 122k → 157k → 103k). No trend, opening range not set.

Waited for the actual trigger. By 08:39 a higher-low sequence had formed
(734.97 → 735.04 → 735.24 → 735.43) with price reclaiming VWAP. Bought at 1.12.

**Had I bought at 08:35 the fill would have been ~1.42. That five-minute wait is
most of the profit.** This is the single most transferable lesson of the day.

### Exit — the owner's, and better than my plan

I was holding for 2.60–2.80 (SPY ~740). SPY topped near 738.7 and rolled over;
by 09:09 the contract was 1.84 bid. **The owner's 2.27 exit was within pennies of
the local high and captured ~$43 more than holding to that point.**

Execution detail worth copying: a 2.22 limit was cancelled at 09:01:47, repriced
to 2.25, and filled at 2.27 with price improvement.

### My error — position reconciliation

At 09:06 I reported "I'm holding" when the position had been closed at 09:02. I
was polling the option **quote** and never re-checked **positions**. Doctrine
section 8 requires reconciling after every action; I reconciled prices, not the
book.

**Fix: every monitoring cycle must call get_option_positions, not just
get_option_quotes.** A quote tells you what a contract is worth, not whether you
own it.

## What worked, in order of contribution

1. **Waiting for the trigger** — ~$30 of the ~$115 gain came from not chasing.
2. **ATR reachability sizing the strike** — picked a target the tape could
   actually reach, rather than the cheapest or furthest contract.
3. **Liquidity gates** — the 0.90% spread meant almost nothing lost to friction,
   against the 40%+ round trips that damaged the account earlier in the month.
4. **Re-scoring direction from scratch** — three days of bearish bias would have
   missed this entirely.
