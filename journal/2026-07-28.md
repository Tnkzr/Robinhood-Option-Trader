### Patterns driving the negative expectancy

**Hold times are far too short for the thesis to resolve.** KO 3 minutes, SMCI 9
minutes, OPEN put 10 minutes, CLF 20 minutes, POET put 21 minutes, SLB 34
minutes, ATAI 60 minutes. Directional option theses need sessions, not minutes.

**Round-trip churn with no edge.** ATAI was bought at $0.15 and sold at $0.15 an
hour later — a pure spread-and-slippage donation with zero thesis change.

**Chasing on entry.** The KO sequence (1.62 → 1.62 → 1.38) and the CLF/SLB
sequence on 7/24 (bought 0.83/0.95, sell orders posted at 1.30/1.55 within five
minutes, both cancelled) show the same reflex: reaching to get in, then
immediately reaching for an exit.

**Small premiums amplify frictions.** On sub-$0.30 contracts the spread is
15–50% of premium. GME ($0.09 → $0.02, −75%) and POET puts ($0.05 → $0.02, −50%)
lost most of their value to structure, not direction.

**Concentration in low-priced speculative names.** OPEN, POET, GME, MARA, RIOT,
ATAI — high-beta, wide-spread underlyings where the option market maker holds
the edge. The three best trades (SLB, XLE, OPEN) were all in liquid, tighter
products.

**Overtrading.** 17 round trips in 18 sessions on a $171 account is action
seeking. The mandate explicitly warns against it.

## Rules adopted going forward

1. **Minimum contract quality.** Bid/ask spread ≤ 10% of mark. Open interest
   ≥ 500. Volume ≥ 100. No exceptions for a "good story."
2. **Never pay above mark.** Enter at mark or better, using a resting limit. If
   it does not fill, the trade was not there. No re-pricing upward to chase.
3. **Position floor.** Do not open a position unless buying power supports a
   contract meeting rule 1. Sub-$0.30 lottery tickets are banned outright.
4. **Minimum hold discipline.** Exit on thesis invalidation or a technical
   level break — not on the mark moving against an inflated entry. If the
   underlying has not moved, there is no new information.
5. **No entries in the last 45 minutes** without a specific closing-auction
   catalyst.
6. **No long premium held through a binary macro event** unless volatility is
   the explicit trade.
7. **Cash is the default.** The bar for deploying is a setup that satisfies the
   mandate's criteria, not the mere existence of buying power.
